        Evidence

        Under the hood.

        Honest status: v0.1, trained on 8 news-reported events. It predicts how many locations flood well. Which ones is only modestly better than chance. Open any section for detail.

        How accurate is it?

        How the model works

        A small logistic regression: how likely a location is to flood given the rainfall and whether it is a junction, road or highway corridor. Small on purpose, so every alert can be explained. Location history and a sector flag were tested and dropped, because they added no signal. Terrain features are next.

        Data and sources

        Every label comes from a named news report. Nothing is inferred. Rainfall is the figure stated in the article (district administration where named).

          Training matrixFlood reportsEventsModel JSON
          What it can’t do yet
          • Small sample. 8 usable events and 110 reported-flooded records.
          • Weak negatives. “Not reported” does not mean dry. News favours highways and well-known junctions.
          • Not the official list. The 159-hotspot names are not public in the sources we found. These 69 locations are news-named.
          • Optimistic candidate set. Each location was chosen because it was reported at least once, which flatters the backtest.
          • Coarse ranking. Locations of the same type share one probability. Terrain data will separate them.
          • Not a safety system. Do not use for real safety decisions.
